## Context

The forces in play: what made this a decision rather than an obvious choice, and
what constraints applied. Enough that a reader who was not there understands the
pressure, without narrating the whole project.

## Decision

What we chose, in the active voice. One decision per record — if this needs an
"and also", it is probably two records.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, hard, or impossible. Include the costs accepted and the
follow-on work it forces, not just the benefits. A record with no downsides is a
record that has not been thought through.
